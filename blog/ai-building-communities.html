AI Building Communities

February 20, 2024

The project that I am working on with the team is not just another AI initiative—it's about creating systems that provide real value that can be used to exponentially increase our ability to develop master planned communities.

As an AI systems engineer on this project, my role is clear: push the limits. I'm developing architectures that are faster, smarter, and more scalable. We're not going to follow trends—we're going to define them.

We have been able to already save countless hours of human runtime by using the initial beta version of our system. Specifically the current version of the system is able to complete a task that would take a human 35-45 minutes in only 1-2 minutes. The next steps are to continue to build out the system and continue to push the limits of what is possible.
