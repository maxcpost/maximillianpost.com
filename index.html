About Me

My current focus is on the intersection of advanced technology and masterplanned community development. More specifically I am working on developing Artificial Intelligence and data analytics algorithms to identify land that is optimal for developing into master planned communities. I am working on this development project in an effort to help alleviate the national(international) housing crisis. I am currently a second year student at the Golisano Institute of Business and Entrepreneurship in Rochester NY, studying to become an expert generalist in business.


My Massive Transformative Purpose: To redefine housing affordability through innovative technologies and sustainable practices, providing a stable foundation for families to thrive.


To learn more about my thoughts and explorations, visit the Blog planet, or check out my code repositories on the Code planet.

Working with friends to push humanity onward and upward.
