About

My focus is on the intersection of technology and master-planned community development. More specifically, I am currently focused on building artificial intelligence and data analytics algorithms to identify land that is optimal for developing into master-planned communities. We are working on this development project in an effort to help alleviate the national housing crisis. I am currently a second-year student at the Golisano Institute of Business and Entrepreneurship in Rochester, NY, studying to become an expert generalist in business.


My Massive Transformative Purpose:To redefine housing affordability through innovative technologies and sustainable practices, providing a stable foundation for families to thrive.
