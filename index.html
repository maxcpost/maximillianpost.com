About Me

I'm Max. I live in Rochester, NY, and graduated from the Golisano Institute of Business and Entrepreneurship.


My current focus is Humanly, where I'm building places where working families can afford to live, have time back in their day, and have the tools and resources to live happier, healthier lives. I've watched friends delay starting families and work second jobs just to cover rent.


I write on the Blog. Code is on GitHub.

Working with friends to push humanity onward and upward.
